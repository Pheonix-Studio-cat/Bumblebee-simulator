BumblebeeSimulator

You are a bumblebee queen founding a colony. Fly the meadow, drink nectar, carry pollen home in your baskets and raise workers before the season closes. Five real species — and each one can reach different flowers.

Being a real bumblebee

  • Your tongue decides your menu. A corolla deeper than your tongue simply cannot be drained. Foxglove is 16 mm deep, and a Fuchsia is 19 mm — which only the giant Patagonian bumblebee can empty. The buff-tailed bumblebee, with its 8 mm tongue, has to bite its way in instead.
  • Cold muscles cannot fly. Below 30 °C in the thorax you are grounded. Shiver with Shift to warm up — it burns energy, so do not waste it.
  • Buzz pollination. Bittersweet nightshade offers no nectar at all and locks its pollen inside tube-shaped anthers. Honeybees cannot get it out; you can, by vibrating.
  • Flower constancy. Visiting the same species again and again pollinates it properly and scores far more.
  • Watch pale flowers. Crab spiders sit on them, colour-matched and waiting.
